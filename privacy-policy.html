Privacy Policy for Rocket Guardian

Information about privacy when you play Rocket Guardian on Android.

Last Updated: September 15, 2026

SHREE STAR ROCK STUDIO ("we," "us," or "our") provides the Android game Rocket Guardian (the "Game"). This Privacy Policy explains what information is handled in connection with the Game and the choices available to you.

Information Collected

Rocket Guardian does not ask you to provide personal information such as your name, postal address, or phone number. The Game uses Google AdMob to display advertising. AdMob and its partners may automatically handle information needed to serve and measure ads, in accordance with Google's policies and your device or advertising settings.

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information through Rocket Guardian.

Device Information

The Game itself does not directly request or maintain a personal profile about you. When advertisements are provided, Google AdMob may process device, app, and advertising information automatically. The exact information and purposes are controlled by Google and may depend on your device settings, consent, and the ads shown.

For details, please review Google's information about partner sites and apps.

Gameplay and Usage Information

We do not directly collect or store a personal record of your gameplay, progress, or in-game interactions for Rocket Guardian. AdMob may receive limited app and ad interaction information as described in Google's applicable policies.

Crash and Diagnostic Information

Rocket Guardian does not use a separate crash-reporting or diagnostic service to collect information from you. If Google AdMob encounters a technical issue while providing an advertisement, Google may process technical information under its own policies.

Advertising and Google AdMob

Rocket Guardian uses Google AdMob for advertising. Google may use information from the device and the app to deliver, limit, personalize, and measure advertisements, subject to applicable law, consent choices, and Google's policies.

Learn more in the Google Privacy Policy and Google's ad settings.

How Information Is Used

Information handled in connection with the Game is used to operate Rocket Guardian and to provide, measure, and improve advertising through Google AdMob. We do not use information to create a separate personal profile for the Game.

Information Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information. Information may be handled by Google AdMob and its service providers as necessary to provide advertising and related measurement. Google handles that information under its own privacy policy.

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to avoid collecting and retaining personal information that is not needed for Rocket Guardian. No method of transmission or electronic storage is completely secure, and third-party services are responsible for the safeguards they apply to information they process.

Data Retention

We do not maintain a separate database of personal information collected directly through Rocket Guardian. Google AdMob may retain information according to its policies and applicable legal requirements.

Children's Privacy

Rocket Guardian does not knowingly request personal information from children. Advertising shown through AdMob is subject to the settings, consent requirements, and protections that Google applies for children and other users where applicable.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can review the request.

Third-Party Services

The third-party service used for advertising in Rocket Guardian is Google AdMob. Third-party services have their own terms and privacy policies, and this policy does not control how they process information.

User Choices

You can review or change available advertising and privacy controls through your Android device settings and Google's ad settings, where those controls are offered. You can also stop using Rocket Guardian by uninstalling it from your device.

Some advertising controls are provided by Google or Android and may vary by device, location, and applicable law.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Game or its services change. The revised policy will be posted on this page with a new "Last Updated" date. Please check this page periodically for updates.
