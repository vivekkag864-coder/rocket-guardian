Account and Data Deletion

How to remove Rocket Guardian data and request help.

Rocket Guardian does not offer user accounts or sign-in, and SHREE STAR ROCK STUDIO does not maintain a personal gameplay profile for the game. Because there is no game account, there is no account to delete.

Delete data stored on your device

Rocket Guardian may keep game settings or progress locally on your Android device. To remove locally stored game data, use either option:

  • Open your Android device's Settings, find Rocket Guardian under Apps, and choose Storage or Storage & cache, then Clear storage or Clear data.
  • Uninstall Rocket Guardian from your device. You can also clear the app's storage before uninstalling.

Menu names and backup behavior can vary by Android version and device. Removing the app does not delete information that may be held by third-party services.

Advertising data

Rocket Guardian uses Google AdMob. Google may process device, app, and advertising information to provide and measure ads under Google's policies. SHREE STAR ROCK STUDIO cannot access or delete information held by Google on your behalf.

For Google's data and advertising controls, visit Google My Ad Center or review Google's Privacy Policy.
