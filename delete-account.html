Delete your Rocket Guardian account

Use this page to request permanent deletion of your Rocket Guardian account and the game data linked to it. You do not need the game installed.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email from the Google account email you use to sign in to Rocket Guardian to sureshchandrakag3@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject Rocket Guardian account deletion request. If you know it, include your pilot name.
  3. We confirm the request comes from the account owner, because it must be sent from the email linked to the account. If we need to check anything, we will reply to that email.
  4. We delete your account and data and email you a confirmation once deletion is complete, within 30 days of your request (usually much sooner).
Email my deletion request

Your request is only treated as complete after we email you the confirmation.

Data that will be deleted

  • Account profile: your email address, Google account ID, pilot name and avatar.
  • Game progress: waves, sectors, save data and settings stored on our server.
  • In-game items: coins, diamonds, keys, Royale tokens, and unlocked skins, guns, ships, drones and upgrades.
  • Scores and leaderboards: your daily, weekly and all-time score board entries.
  • Missions and rewards: mission, achievement, challenge, discovery and daily-reward progress.
  • Multiplayer: your 1v1, co-op and PvP stats and match history.
  • Sessions: your sign-in sessions on every device.

Deletion is permanent. Purchased items and Star Pass benefits linked to the account cannot be restored.

Data we keep, and for how long

  • Your deletion request email and our confirmation reply are kept for 12 months as a record that the request was handled, then deleted.
  • Purchase records held by Google Play for coins, diamonds and Star Pass are kept by Google under its own policies. We cannot delete them. Manage them at play.google.com/store/account.
  • Advertising data is handled by Google AdMob under Google's policies. You can reset or limit it in your Android or Google ad settings.
Subscriptions: deleting your account does not cancel Google Play billing. If you have Star Pass, cancel it in Google Play › Payments & subscriptions.

Guest players

If you only played as a Guest, there is no email linked to your progress. Your guest data is stored on your phone. Delete it in the game under Settings › Reset Save, or uninstall the app. If you played online as a guest and want your pilot name removed from the score boards, email us your pilot name.
